Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nova Hub ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address (specifically .edu emails for verification), password, major, and campus.
  • Profile Information: Biography and profile pictures.
  • User-Generated Content: Posts, comments, likes, and direct chat messages.
  • Connections: Friend requests sent, received, and accepted.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the app.
  • Verify your student status using your college email domain.
  • Enable user-to-user communications (chats, comments).
  • Enhance profile and post images (using third-party APIs).

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our app:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, real-time database hosting (Firestore), and cloud storage.
  • Hugging Face API: Used temporarily to process and enhance image quality. Images sent to this API are not permanently stored by us outside of your Firestore document.

5. Data Security

We implement strict security measures, including Firebase Security Rules, to ensure that your private chats are only visible to participants and that your account can only be modified by you.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, edit, or delete your profile information, posts, and comments directly within the app. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, please contact us.
