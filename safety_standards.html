Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Our Commitment to Safety

Nova Hub is dedicated to providing a safe, respectful, and secure environment for all college students. As a social platform, we have a zero-tolerance policy for any content or behavior that threatens the safety of our users, particularly minors.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE (Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation)

Nova Hub strictly prohibits any content that depicts, encourages, or promotes Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing images or videos depicting the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any predatory behavior directed at minors.
  • Links to external sites hosting CSAM/CSAE content.

Any user found violating this policy will face immediate and permanent account suspension.

3. Law Enforcement Cooperation

We are legally and morally obligated to report any instances of CSAM or CSAE to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement. We fully cooperate with law enforcement investigations regarding child safety.

4. Reporting & Moderation

User safety relies on community vigilance. We strongly encourage users to immediately report any suspicious, abusive, or explicit content.

  • In-App Reporting: You can report a user or post directly within the Nova Hub app.
  • Direct Email: You can report severe safety concerns directly to our moderation team via email.

Our moderation team reviews reports promptly and takes decisive action, which may include content removal, account deletion, and reporting to authorities.
